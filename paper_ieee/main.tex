\begin{abstract}
This paper investigates neural phase retrieval for coherent diffraction imaging.
We first reproduce an AutoPhaseNN-style pipeline and then replace the backbone
with a UMamba-based model while keeping the data flow, Fourier-domain
post-processing, and evaluation protocol aligned. Preliminary experiments show
that Fourier-domain losses alone can be misleading: models may obtain competitive
diffraction fitting metrics while producing poor real-space amplitude, phase, or
support reconstructions. We analyze this gap and discuss the role of spatial
support priors in neural phase retrieval.
\end{abstract}
